### What is this?

Browse and filter nine Adobe scripts: four for InDesign, three for Illustrator, and two for Photoshop. Download the ones you need and follow the installation guidance.

### How does it work?

The catalogue and downloads are bundled with Robot. Per-card download-click counts are stored in this browser's `localStorage`, not verified installation or successful-run counts. The module does not send those counts to a server.
