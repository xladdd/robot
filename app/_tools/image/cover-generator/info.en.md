### What is this?

Choose a textbook audience and subject, add optional keywords and reference covers, and request two or four concepts. You will get distinct, text-free cover artworks for later typography and layout in InDesign.

### How does it work?

Your choices and optional references are sent through OpenRouter to `mistralai/mistral-medium-3-5 🇪🇺`, which writes two or four distinct structured concepts with one or two hero subjects and up to two supporting objects. The app deterministically serializes each concept as concise JSON with `subjects` first, places every focal object as a compact cluster near the bottom edge, and sends that exact JSON unchanged to the selected image model. A wide environmental composition keeps the upper half as a softly detailed, naturally continuing part of the same environment for later typography. The default house medium is premium editorial photomontage or a premium montage of detailed sophisticated stylized illustrations; an explicit medium keyword such as cartoon or illustration overrides it. If Medium cannot serve the request, OpenRouter may use `mistralai/ministral-14b-2512 🇪🇺`. The generated images and a report can then be downloaded together.
