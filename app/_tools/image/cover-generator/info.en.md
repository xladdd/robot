### What is this?

Choose a textbook audience, visual treatment, and subject, add optional keywords and reference images, and request one, two, or four concepts. You will get distinct, text-free cover artworks for later typography and layout in InDesign.

### How does it work?

Your audience, subject, selected visual treatment, and optional keywords are sent through OpenRouter to `mistralai/mistral-medium-3-5 🇪🇺`, which writes one, two, or four distinct structured concepts with one or two hero subjects and one to three supporting objects. The app serializes each concept as concise JSON with `subjects` first and a deterministic `composition` object. That object makes the artwork edge-to-edge, gives focal elements substantial presence across the lower and middle field, and keeps the upper third calm for typography while the same background, atmosphere, light, texture, and broad forms continue naturally through it. Optional reference images bypass the planner and are supplied directly to the selected image model as visual guidance for palette, atmosphere, material finish, and treatment. With Automatic selected, Medium chooses a subject-appropriate treatment. Any explicit dropdown selection deterministically controls the early style and treatment fields sent to the image model, including photographic/editorial montage, scientific material study, detailed illustration montage, coloured wood engraving, modern risograph, ink-lined watercolour, and 20th century-style propaganda watercolour. If Medium cannot serve the planning request, OpenRouter may use `mistralai/ministral-14b-2512 🇪🇺`. The generated images and a report can then be downloaded together.
