### What is this?

Choose a textbook audience and subject, add optional keywords and reference covers, and request two or four concepts. You will get distinct, text-free cover artworks for later typography and layout in InDesign.

### How does it work?

Your choices and optional references are sent through OpenRouter to `mistralai/mistral-medium-3-5 🇪🇺`, which writes two or four distinct, concise image-ready prompts. If Medium cannot serve the request, OpenRouter may use `mistralai/ministral-14b-2512 🇪🇺`; if neither planner responds, generation stops rather than inventing local directions. Each prompt is sent unchanged to the selected image model: `black-forest-labs/flux.2-pro 🇩🇪` or `google/gemini-3.1-flash-lite-image 🇺🇸`, both at 1K. The generated images and a report can then be downloaded together.
