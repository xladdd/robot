### What is this?

Choose a textbook audience, visual treatment, and subject, add optional keywords and reference images, and request one, two, or four concepts. You will get distinct, text-free cover artworks for later typography and layout in InDesign.

### How does it work?

Audience, subject, treatment, and keywords go to the planner through OpenRouter, with a backup planner if needed. Robot builds distinct structured concepts with a calm upper area for later typography. Reference images bypass the planner and go directly to the selected artwork model. Composition instructions guide the model but do not guarantee placement. Download the artwork and report, then review image details before publication.
