### What is this?

Map Maker creates editable SVG maps for print and editorial work. It currently opens at the present year: the year controls are intentionally locked, although the historical API and bundled timeline data already exist, so time travel is not yet available in the interface. Inside the orange crop, drag to pan and use the wheel or buttons to zoom. Use the political, physical, historical, climate, or blank layer presets, then refine individual layers. Essential, Standard, and Detailed label density controls feed the collision layout; **Position labels** lets you drag individual labels and **Hide labels** removes selected labels. **Draw** adds symbols, text, and routes.

Choose a 90 mm column, 135 mm half-page, or 180 mm full-page preset to export physical type sizes, line weights, and legend dimensions. Add a title, subtitle, source, projection note, and copyright before export. Publication preflight flags print size, source/date omissions, RGB colours, unavailable Verdana, labels crossing the crop, dense layer combinations, and mixed-period overlays. **Download SVG** exports the visible crop with edits, named layers, publication metadata, and physical dimensions.

The prompt field is active and AI-backed through OpenRouter. When you apply a prompt, the server sends your instruction, the current map year, and the names of territories available for that year. AI interprets that limited context into constrained territory fills and simple labels. Review each proposed operation and select which ones to apply; applied and dismissed requests remain in session history. Common English, Czech, and historical territory aliases are normalized before exact resolution. AI does not render the map, and model-generated polygons are not accepted. Application code deterministically renders the geometry, projection, layers, crop, and SVG from bundled map data.

### How does it work?

The base map, lakes, rivers, and present-day countries use bundled Natural Earth 1:10m data. Historical support uses Cliopatria before 1886, CShapes from 1886 through its 1 January 2018 snapshot, and present-day geometry for 2019–2026. For years before 1000, the cities layer can use settlements from the Pleiades gazetteer that existed in the selected year. Optional terrain, mountain, summit, river, lake, disputed-boundary, and climate overlays may describe present-day or reference-period conditions rather than the selected political year. The Physical preset combines restrained terrain tinting with editable, projection-aware relief hachures clipped to Natural Earth mountain ranges, ranked Natural Earth summit triangles and elevation labels, and a four-segment graphic scale. The hachures are a cartographic relief treatment, not elevation-derived hillshade. Climate uses five editable contour paths derived from the Beck et al. 1980–2016 high-resolution classification; its colour key appears in the map legend when enabled. Source coverage, names, dates, and boundaries can be incomplete or disputed. Review the exported map editorially and in your print workflow before publication.
