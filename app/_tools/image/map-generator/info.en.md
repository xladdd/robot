### What is this?

Choose a year, move and zoom the world map, switch layers, and optionally apply an Adobe Swatch Exchange palette. Export an editable SVG with the visible crop, colours, labels, and named layers. There is no prompt field or AI generation in the current map interface.

### How does it work?

Deterministic rendering uses bundled data: Cliopatria before 1886; CShapes from 1886 through its 1 January 2018 snapshot; present-day geometry for 2019–2026. Physical layers are not reconstructed for the selected historical year. Climate represents a 1980–2016 reference period, not historical climate for that year. Natural Earth and world-atlas supply additional geography. Review historical boundaries, coverage, and source limitations before publication.
