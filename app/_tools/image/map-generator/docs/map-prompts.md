# Physical geography

Prompt: "Create a factual map of South America showing national borders and labeling every country. Also label the Andes Mountains, Amazon River, Amazon Basin, Atacama Desert, Brazilian Highlands, Pacific Ocean, and Atlantic Ocean. Include a scale bar, north arrow, and legend."

# Thematic geography

Prompt: "Create a map of Europe showing the major climate regions: Mediterranean, oceanic, continental, subarctic, tundra, and mountain climate. Use separate filled regions, label major seas and selected countries for orientation, and include a legend."

# Historical political map

Prompt: "Create a map of Europe in 1914 immediately before the First World War. Show national borders and label the major states, including the German Empire, Austro-Hungarian Empire, Russian Empire, Ottoman Empire, France, United Kingdom, Italy, Serbia, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, Belgium, and the Netherlands. Distinguish the Triple Entente and Central Powers with a legend."

# Historical expansion / movement map

Prompt: "Create a historical map showing the expansion of the Roman Republic and Roman Empire around the Mediterranean. Distinguish territory controlled by 264 BCE, 44 BCE, 14 CE, and 117 CE. Label Rome, Carthage, Alexandria, Constantinople, the Mediterranean Sea, and major provinces, with a chronological legend."

# Route-heavy historical map

Prompt: "Create a map of the Mediterranean and Near East showing the main routes of Alexander the Great’s campaigns from Macedonia to Egypt and India between 334 and 323 BCE. Mark and label Pella, Granicus, Issus, Tyre, Alexandria, Gaugamela, Babylon, Persepolis, Bactria, and the Hydaspes River. Use arrows for campaign routes, mark major battles separately, show relevant ancient regions, and include a legend."
