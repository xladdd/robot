These files are browser assets for Index Creator's local OCR fallback.

- Tesseract.js and tesseract.js-core: Apache License 2.0.
- Czech `ces.traineddata.gz`: distributed by the `@tesseract.js-data/ces` package.

The OCR runs in the browser. Uploaded PDFs, rendered page images, and OCR text are not sent to OpenRouter or any other server.
